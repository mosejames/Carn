Three contracts. One ward. Four months.

College Park is voting Monday on a $72,800 a year Chief of Staff for Ward 1. It is the third staff contract for that one office since April, and it is on the consent agenda, which means it passes in a block with no discussion.

The question nobody has answered

What did the city get?

Ward 1 ran for decades without any of this. Ambrose Clay held the seat until the end of 2023. In the city's published agendas, no Ward 1 staff contract appears before 2026.
Nobody has said what the April aide cost. The April 20 agenda lists the contract with no dollar figure at all. Four months later it is being replaced.
Nobody has said what any of it produced. No report, no measure, no before and after has come to council with these renewals.
Nobody has said why the city's own departments cannot do it. College Park staffs Procurement, Finance, the City Clerk, Customer Service, and Strategic Communications and Public Information right now.

Monday night

Ask them to pull 8.C from consent.

Every question below points at a document, so it is hard to answer with a speech.

  1. What did the city get for the first two contracts? April 20 and August 3, both Ward 1, both consent, both on G/L 100-1100-52-6170. Show the work.
  2. Is the Events Coordinator still being paid after Monday? It was approved fourteen days ago at $2,727.27 a month. If it continues, Ward 1 staffing is $105,527 a year.
  3. Which city department was asked whether it could do this work? Name them, and say what they answered.
  4. Why is a $291,199.68 contract on the consent agenda? Section 8 is Consent. Section 9 is Regular Business. This is in section 8.
  5. Who supervises this position? Exhibit A (h) puts it outside every city officer. Charter Sec. 4-7(c) gives the city manager control over all departments and divisions.
  6. Does a future council have to vote again to keep paying? Contract Sec. 2 renews it automatically for three more years, with no notice window stated.
